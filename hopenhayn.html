Firm Dynamics — Stationary Equilibrium

Hopenhayn (1992)

Each period, firms with productivity below the exit cutoff z* leave the market. New entrants replace them, drawing from the entry distribution ν. The overall distribution remains unchanged — this is what it means for the equilibrium to be stationary.

Key insight: The exit cutoff z* is determined by the fixed cost c_f — not the entry cost c_e. Changing c_e alone shifts the equilibrium price and the mass of entrants, but leaves z* unchanged. The entry distribution ν is the stationary distribution of the productivity process and is identical across all scenarios.
Calibration: β=0.8, θ=2/3, w=1, D=100. Fixed cost c_f, entry cost c_e, ρ_z=0.9, σ_z=0.2. Rouwenhorst discretisation (N=20 nodes).
